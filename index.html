04About Regular Gears

Great gear shouldn't be hard to find in Cambodia.

Regular Gears started with a simple frustration: the best keyboards, mouse and headsets were always out of stock, overpriced, or only available overseas. So we started bringing them in ourselves, one carefully chosen product at a time.

Today we supply students building their first desk setup, competitive players chasing every millisecond, and enthusiasts who just love a good-sounding keyboard. Different people, same standard: gear we would put on our own desks.
